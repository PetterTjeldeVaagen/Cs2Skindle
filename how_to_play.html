How to play

Daily challenge:

The Skindle Daily Challenge is played by searching for and guessing a Counter-Strike skin using the search bar. When you select a skin, it is added to the table on the right, where its attributes are displayed. If your guess shares any attributes with the correct skin, those cells will turn green. For the release year, an arrow indicates whether your guess is older or newer than the correct answer. The goal is to guess the correct skin in as few attempts as possible. The daily skin is the same for everyone, so you can challenge your friends and compare your skin knowledge.

Freeplay:

Freeplay works the same way as the Daily Challenge, except the skin changes every game. You can also choose different difficulty levels based on skin rarity.

Skin grid:

Skin Grid is the newest addition to the site. You win by filling in skins that meet both conditions for each cell. Difficulty can vary greatly from game to game, but every grid is always possible to complete (unless something breaks). If you have suggestions for new conditions, feel free to send them to the email below—feedback is always appreciated.

Skin-Tac-Toe:

Skin-Tac-Toe is a fun twist on the classic tic-tac-toe game. Instead of X's and O's, players take turns placing skins on the grid. The first player to get three skins in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins the game. It's a great way to test your skin knowledge while enjoying a familiar game format. Currently only available as a two-player local game, but online multiplayer may be added in the future.
